# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the Simple Interest Calculator project.

All contributors are expected to treat others with respect, regardless of their background, experience, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Collaborating professionally and respectfully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments.
- Personal attacks, insults, or threats.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any behavior that is inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues and Pull Requests
- Code reviews
- Project discussions
- Other communication related to this project

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately through an available contact method on their GitHub profile or use GitHub's reporting tools.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

## Our Commitment

We believe that collaboration, respect, and open communication are essential to successful open-source development.

By participating in this project, contributors agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
